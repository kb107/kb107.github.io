Hi! I'm currently a predoctoral fellow at the <a href='https://kiac.iisc.ac.in/'>Kotak IISc AI/ML Centre</a>, working in the <a href='https://sites.google.com/view/prathosh/home?authuser=0'>Representation Learning Lab</a> at the Department of Electrical Communication Engineering, <a href='https://iisc.ac.in/'>IISc Bangalore</a>, under the supervision of <a href='https://sites.google.com/view/prathosh/home?authuser=0'>Dr. Prathosh AP</a>. My current research focuses on **improving zero-shot adversarial robustness in vision-language models (VLMs) using test-time prompt tuning strategies**. I’ve also recently contributed to work on **domain generalisation in medical imaging using Langevin-based data augmentation**, which is currently under review.

I completed my B.Tech. in Electrical Engineering from <a href='https://www.iitkgp.ac.in/'>IIT Kharagpur</a>, during which I had the opportunity to work at the <a href='https://sites.google.com/view/prathosh/home?authuser=0'>Representation Learning Lab, IISc</a> on multiple occasions. My earlier work involved **unsupervised domain adaptation in medical imaging using energy-based models (EBMs) for image-to-image translation**, which led to a co-authored publication in Elsevier's <a href='https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/medical-image-analysis'>Medical Image Analysis</a>. Later, I explored topics around multi-concept compositionality and catastrophic neglect in diffusion-based text-to-image generation, working with latent consistency models (LCMs) and attention regularisation techniques at inference time.

In the summer of 2023, I interned at the <a href='https://vision-and-learning-lab-ualberta.github.io/'>Vision and Learning Lab</a> at the <a href='https://www.ualberta.ca/en/index.html'>University of Alberta</a> with <a href='https://www.ece.ualberta.ca/~lcheng5/'>Dr. Li Cheng</a>, supported by the <a href='https://www.mitacs.ca/our-programs/globalink-research-internship-students/'>Mitacs GRI Fellowship</a>. There, I worked on building a multi-modal human motion dataset, including multi-sensor RGB-D calibration and explored monocular SLAM for eye-tracking pose estimation. I'm **broadly interested in problems at the intersection of representation learning, robustness, and multimodal intelligence**, and am **currently looking for research assistant positions or long-term internships at machine learning and visual computing research groups**. If my work aligns with your interests, feel free to check out my <a href='https://drive.google.com/file/d/12hkypji-MFJQyklcAv4XPT3W3Z2tW0dt/view?usp=sharing'>CV</a> and don't hesitate to reach out via email at kinjawl03@gmail.com or <a href='https://www.linkedin.com/in/kinjawl-bhattacharyya-60bb151b2/'>LinkedIn</a>.
