Hi! I am an incoming PhD student in Computer Science at <a href="https://www.unc.edu/">UNC Chapel Hill</a>, where I will be joining <a href="https://www.cs.unc.edu/~ronisen/">Prof. Roni Sengupta's</a> lab. My research will focus on physics-aware generative models for sim2real transfer and 3D perception, with applications to medical imaging and endoscopy videos.

I graduated in 2024 with a B.Tech. in Electrical Engineering from the <a href="https://www.iitkgp.ac.in/">Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kharagpur</a>. Broadly, I am interested in using generative models to build learning systems that <b><em>reason and generalize beyond controlled training settings</em></b> and can <b><em>communicate their uncertainty and limitations</em></b>.

Previously, I worked in the <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/prathosh/home">Representation Learning Lab</a> at <a href="https://iisc.ac.in/">IISc Bangalore</a> with <a href="https://eecs.iisc.ac.in/people/prathosh-a-p/">Prof. Prathosh A. P.</a> on robustness and domain generalization for medical imaging, resulting in publications in <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1361841523002918?via%3Dihub">Medical Image Analysis</a> and at <a href="https://backpropagator.github.io/LangDAug/">ICML 2025</a>. I also interned at the <a href="https://vision-and-learning-lab-ualberta.github.io/">Vision and Learning Lab</a>, <a href="https://www.ualberta.ca/en/index.html">University of Alberta</a>, supported by the <a href="https://www.mitacs.ca/our-programs/globalink-research-internship-students/">Mitacs Globalink Research Internship</a>. More recently, I was a Research Intern at the <a href="https://www.palmlab.org/">PALM Lab</a> at the <a href="https://www.usf.edu/">University of South Florida</a>, where I worked with <a href="https://www.anshumanc.com/">Prof. Anshuman Chhabra</a> on reliability in language models, including abstention, uncertainty, and hallucination reduction.

You can find more details in my <a href="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1UjnZlObr-6awAPJKRG838FQPsmN7JLhi/view?usp=sharing">CV</a> and <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=Uc__Y2cAAAAJ">Google Scholar profile</a>.
