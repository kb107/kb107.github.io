Hi! I’m currently a Research Intern in the <a href="https://www.palmlab.org/">PALM Lab</a> at the
<a href="https://www.usf.edu/">University of South Florida</a>, working with
<a href="https://www.anshumanc.com/">Prof. Anshuman Chhabra</a> on reliability for language models, focusing on abstention, epistemic uncertainty, and reducing overconfident hallucinations using data-centric methods and, more recently, RLVR.

I graduated in 2024 with a B.Tech. in Electrical Engineering from the <a href="https://www.iitkgp.ac.in/">Indian Institute of Technology(IIT) Kharagpur</a>.
Broadly, I’m interested in building learning systems that can <b><em>reason and generalize well</em></b> and can <b><em>communicate their limits</em></b>.
My work spans robust/domain generalization (often via generative modeling, e.g., EBMs and diffusion/consistency models),
as well as interpretability and representation learning for multimodal generation.

Previously, I worked in the <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/prathosh/home">Representation Learning Lab</a> at
<a href="https://iisc.ac.in/">IISc Bangalore</a> with <a href="https://eecs.iisc.ac.in/people/prathosh-a-p/">Prof. Prathosh A P</a>,
including projects on medical imaging robustness (with publications in
<a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1361841523002918?via%3Dihub">Medical Image Analysis</a> and an ICML poster).
I also interned at the <a href="https://vision-and-learning-lab-ualberta.github.io/">Vision and Learning Lab</a>,
<a href="https://www.ualberta.ca/en/index.html">University of Alberta</a>, supported by the
<a href="https://www.mitacs.ca/our-programs/globalink-research-internship-students/">Mitacs Globalink Research Internship</a>.

I’m applying for PhD programs (Fall 2026) in <em>trustworthy &amp; robust machine learning</em>.
I’m also open to research internships / RA roles (6–12 months, remote or onsite) aligned with <em>model reliability</em> and
<em>robust generalization</em>.
